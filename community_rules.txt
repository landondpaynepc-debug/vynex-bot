# Vynex Tweaks — Community Rules

*Welcome to Vynex Tweaks! Our goal is to provide a helpful, safe, and friendly community for gamers interested in PC optimization, performance tweaks, and improving their overall gaming experience.*

*By remaining in this server, you agree to follow the rules listed below. Please take a moment to read through them, as violations may result in moderation action.*

*1. Respect Everyone*
> Treat all members with respect. Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, and unnecessary drama are not tolerated.

*2. No Spamming*
> Do not spam messages, emojis, mentions, or commands. Keep conversations in the appropriate channels.

*3. No Advertising*
> Do not advertise other servers, products, services, or social media without staff permission.

*4. Keep Content Appropriate*
> No NSFW content, gore, inappropriate media, or anything that violates Discord's Terms of Service.

*5. No Scamming or Malicious Files*
> Scamming, phishing, malware, suspicious downloads, and attempts to steal personal information are strictly prohibited.

*6. Safe Tweaking & Optimization*
> Do not share harmful scripts or misleading tweaks. Explain what your files do, avoid false performance claims, and encourage users to back up their systems before making changes.

*7. No Impersonation*
> Do not impersonate staff, developers, or other members. Do not falsely claim to represent Vynex Tweaks.

*8. Protect Privacy*
> Do not share private information, leak personal details, or expose private conversations without permission.

*9. Follow Staff Instructions*
> Respect moderators and administrators. If you disagree with a decision, open a support ticket instead of arguing publicly.

*10. Use Channels Correctly*
> Post in the appropriate channels, provide useful details when requesting support, and be patient while waiting for a response.

*11. No Exploiting or Rule Evasion*
> Do not exploit bots, abuse server features, evade punishments with alternate accounts, or attempt unauthorized access to accounts or systems.

*12. Common Sense*
> Staff may take action against behavior that harms the community, even if a specific situation is not directly listed above.

# Advertising Products & Services

> Advertising, selling, or promoting products and services within Vynex Tweaks must follow the rules below. These guidelines are in place to protect our community, our products, and our members from unauthorized sales, scams, and misleading promotions.

*1. Unauthorized Selling & Reselling*
> Do not sell, resell, redistribute, or share paid Vynex Tweaks products without explicit authorization from the Vynex Tweaks team. This includes tweaks, optimization packs, scripts, configurations, and other paid resources. Unauthorized distribution or resale may result in a permanent ban.

*2. Ownership & Credit*
> Do not claim ownership of another person's work, including scripts, tweaks, configurations, tools, or other digital resources. Always give proper credit to the original creator when sharing their work, and never remove ownership notices or attempt to pass someone else's work off as your own.

*3. Leaked or Stolen Content*
> Sharing, selling, requesting, or distributing leaked, stolen, cracked, or otherwise unauthorized paid content is strictly prohibited. This includes content obtained without the creator's permission or distributed in violation of its licensing terms. Claiming that a leak is harmless does not make its distribution acceptable.

*4. Advertising & Self-Promotion*
> Do not advertise third-party tweak services, competing products, Discord servers, websites, or other businesses without prior staff approval. This also applies to promotional links, referral codes, discount codes, and unsolicited advertisements sent through direct messages to community members. Contact staff if you wish to request permission to promote a legitimate service.

*5. Official Sales & Partnerships*
> All official sales, partnerships, sponsorships, giveaways, and promotional campaigns involving Vynex Tweaks must be approved by the appropriate staff members. Do not announce partnerships, offer unofficial discounts, promise rewards, or represent yourself as an authorized seller without permission from the Vynex Tweaks team.

*6. Scams & Fraudulent Transactions*
> Fraudulent sales, fake payment confirmations, chargeback abuse, phishing links, misleading offers, and attempts to obtain money or products through deception are prohibited. Sellers must accurately represent what they are offering, including its features, limitations, pricing, and delivery terms. Any suspected scam should be reported to staff immediately.

*7. Misleading Claims & False Advertising*
> Do not make false or unsupported claims about a product's performance, safety, features, or results. Do not guarantee FPS increases, reduced input delay, or other performance improvements without reliable evidence. Any product or service promoted within the community must be represented honestly and transparently.

*8. Unauthorized Use of the Vynex Tweaks Brand*
> Do not use the Vynex Tweaks name, logo, branding, or official materials to promote personal products or services in a way that suggests endorsement or affiliation without permission. Do not create fake official stores, impersonate authorized sellers, or mislead members into believing an unofficial product is an official Vynex Tweaks release.

*9. Reporting Violations*
> If you encounter unauthorized sales, stolen content, suspicious links, or potentially fraudulent transactions, report the matter to the moderation team through the appropriate support channel. Provide relevant evidence when possible, and avoid publicly sharing sensitive payment details or personal information.

# Moderation & Punishments

> Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, staff may issue:
>
> Verbal Warning — A reminder to follow the rules.
>
> Official Warning — A formal warning for a rule violation.
>
> Timeout — Temporary restriction from communicating in the server.
>
> Kick — Removal from the server, with the possibility of rejoining.
>
> Temporary Ban — Removal for a specified period.
>
> Permanent Ban — Removal from the community without an expected return.
>
> *Severe violations, including scams, malicious file distribution, doxxing, credible threats, and ban evasion, may result in an immediate ban without prior warning.*
>
> *Punishments are based on the severity of the violation, the circumstances, and any relevant previous offenses. Staff reserve the right to determine an appropriate response.*



# Final Notice

> *By participating in Vynex Tweaks, you acknowledge that you are expected to follow these rules. If you witness a violation, report it to the moderation team rather than escalating the situation yourself.*

> *Thank you for being part of Vynex Tweaks. We appreciate everyone who helps keep this community safe, respectful, and focused on better PC performance.*
