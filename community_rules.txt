# Vynex Tweaks — Community Rules

*Welcome to Vynex Tweaks! Our goal is to provide a helpful, safe, and friendly community for gamers interested in PC optimization, performance tweaks, and improving their overall gaming experience.*

*By remaining in this server, you agree to follow the rules listed below. Please take a moment to read through them, as violations may result in moderation action.*

*1. Respect Everyone*
> Treat all members with respect, regardless of their experience, opinions, or skill level.
>
> Harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, and personal attacks are not allowed.
>
> Do not start arguments or intentionally provoke other members.
>
> Keep disagreements civil. If an issue gets out of hand, contact a staff member instead of escalating it.
>
> Jokes are fine, but do not use them as an excuse to target or harass someone.

*2. No Spam or Chat Abuse*

> Do not spam messages, emojis, stickers, GIFs, or reactions.
>
> Avoid excessive caps, repeated messages, and unnecessary mentions.
>
> Do not flood channels with unrelated messages.
>
> Do not ping staff or other members repeatedly without a valid reason.
>
> Keep conversations in their appropriate channels.

*3. No Advertising or Self-Promotion*

> Advertising other Discord servers, websites, services, or social media accounts without staff permission is prohibited.
>
> Do not send unsolicited advertisements through direct messages to members of this community.
>
> Do not promote competing tweak services or products without authorization.
>
> Referral links, invite links, and promotional codes must be approved by staff before being posted.

*4. Keep Content Appropriate*

> No NSFW, sexually explicit, excessively graphic, or otherwise inappropriate content.
>
> No gore, shock content, or disturbing media.
>
> Do not post content intended to make other members uncomfortable.
>
> Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times.

*5. No Scams or Malicious Files*

> Scamming, phishing, impersonation, and attempts to steal accounts or personal information are strictly prohibited.
>
> Never distribute malware, viruses, credential stealers, or other malicious software.
>
> Do not share suspicious downloads or links that could compromise another member's device.
>
> Do not falsely advertise products, claim fake results, or mislead members into purchasing a service.
>
> Any suspicious files or links should be reported to staff immediately.

*6. Tweaks, Scripts & PC Safety*

> Only download tweaks, scripts, and optimization tools from sources you trust.
>
> Do not distribute malicious, destructive, or secretly harmful scripts.
>
> Clearly explain what a shared script does and disclose any significant system changes it makes.
>
> Do not present risky registry edits, debloat scripts, or system modifications as completely safe without proper evidence.
>
> Do not pressure members into running commands they do not understand.
>
> Never ask members to share passwords, authentication codes, recovery keys, or other sensitive credentials.
>
> Vynex Tweaks is not responsible for damage caused by third-party files or modifications shared by members. Always back up important data before changing system settings.

*7. No False Claims or Misleading Advice*

> Do not make fake claims about FPS increases, reduced latency, or improved system performance.
>
> Avoid guaranteeing results that depend on a user's hardware, software, or configuration.
>
> Do not intentionally provide incorrect technical advice to mislead other members.
>
> When sharing benchmarks or performance results, provide accurate information and relevant system specifications where possible.
>
> Respect that different PCs may respond differently to the same optimization.

*8. Support & Help Channels*

> Use the appropriate channel when requesting help.
>
> Provide useful information about your PC, including relevant hardware, Windows version, and the issue you are experiencing.
>
> Be patient while waiting for support. Staff and community members may not always be available immediately.
>
> Do not repeatedly open tickets for the same issue.
>
> Do not argue with support staff because a solution does not work for your particular setup.
>
> Never share passwords, private account information, or sensitive personal data in support tickets.
>
*9. No Impersonation*
>
> Do not impersonate Vynex Tweaks staff, developers, partners, or other community members.
>
> Do not use misleading names, profile pictures, or identities to deceive others.
>
> Do not falsely claim to be affiliated with Vynex Tweaks.
>
> Report suspicious accounts pretending to represent the community.

*10. No Drama or Unnecessary Disputes*

> Do not bring personal disputes into public channels.

> Avoid starting arguments over other communities, services, or individual members.

> Do not encourage dogpiling, targeted harassment, or mass reporting.

> If you have a concern about a member or staff decision, raise it privately through the appropriate support channel.

*11. Privacy & Personal Information*

> Do not share another person's private information without their explicit permission.
>
> Doxxing, threats to expose personal information, and attempts to identify anonymous members are prohibited.
>
> Do not publish private messages, tickets, or staff discussions without authorization when doing so would expose private information.
>
> Respect the privacy of everyone in the community.

*12. Voice Channel Rules*

> Do not mic spam, deafen spam, or repeatedly join and leave channels to disrupt others.
>
> Avoid excessively loud audio, soundboards, and background noise.
>
> Do not interrupt support sessions or intentionally disrupt conversations.
>
> Follow staff instructions if a voice channel becomes disruptive.

*13. Advertising Products & Services*

> Do not sell, resell, or distribute paid Vynex Tweaks products without authorization.
>
> Do not claim ownership of another person's scripts, tweaks, or other work.
>
> Do not distribute leaked, stolen, or unauthorized paid content.
>
> Any official sales, partnerships, giveaways, or promotions must follow the instructions provided by Vynex Tweaks staff.
>
> Fraudulent transactions and deceptive sales practices are prohibited.

*14. Follow Discord's Rules*

> All members must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
>
> Do not use this server to coordinate malicious activity, evade platform restrictions, or engage in prohibited behavior.
>
> Attempts to exploit Discord features to disrupt the community are not allowed.

*15. Listen to Staff*

> Follow reasonable instructions from authorized Vynex Tweaks moderators and administrators.
>
> Do not interfere with active moderation or intentionally disrupt staff investigations.
>
> If you disagree with a moderation decision, appeal through the appropriate channel rather than arguing publicly.
>
> Do not impersonate staff or claim to have permissions you do not possess.
>
> Staff members are expected to enforce these rules fairly and consistently.

*16. No Rule Evasion*

> Do not use alternate accounts to evade mutes, bans, or other moderation actions.
>
> Do not delete and repost content to bypass moderation.
>
> Do not encourage other members to break rules on your behalf.
>
> Attempts to evade punishments may result in additional action.

*17. No Exploitation or Abuse*

> Do not exploit bots, tickets, verification systems, or other server features.
>
> Do not abuse commands, loopholes, or permissions to disrupt the community.
>
> Do not attempt to gain unauthorized access to accounts, systems, or private resources.
>
> Report security vulnerabilities or serious technical issues to staff privately.

*18. Use the Correct Channels*

> Read channel descriptions before posting.
>
> Keep support requests, general discussion, media, suggestions, and announcements in their designated channels.
>
> Do not post the same question or content across multiple channels.
>
> Follow any additional instructions posted in individual channels.

*19. Giveaways & Community Events*

> Follow the instructions and eligibility requirements for official giveaways and events.
>
> Do not use alternate accounts to claim multiple entries or rewards.
>
> Do not manipulate entries, results, or voting systems.
>
> Report suspicious giveaway messages or fake prize announcements to staff.

*20. Common Sense Applies*

> These rules cover the most common situations, but they cannot account for every possible scenario.
>
> Deliberately exploiting loopholes or engaging in behavior that seriously harms the community may still result in moderation action.
>
> Staff may take action against behavior that threatens member safety, server security, or the integrity of Vynex Tweaks.

# Moderation & Punishments

> Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, staff may issue:
>
> Verbal Warning — A reminder to follow the rules.
>
> Official Warning — A formal warning for a rule violation.
>
> Timeout — Temporary restriction from communicating in the server.
>
> Kick — Removal from the server, with the possibility of rejoining.
>
> Temporary Ban — Removal for a specified period.
>
> Permanent Ban — Removal from the community without an expected return.
>
> *Severe violations, including scams, malicious file distribution, doxxing, credible threats, and ban evasion, may result in an immediate ban without prior warning.*
>
> *Punishments are based on the severity of the violation, the circumstances, and any relevant previous offenses. Staff reserve the right to determine an appropriate response.*



# Final Notice

> *By participating in Vynex Tweaks, you acknowledge that you are expected to follow these rules. If you witness a violation, report it to the moderation team rather than escalating the situation yourself.*

> *Thank you for being part of Vynex Tweaks. We appreciate everyone who helps keep this community safe, respectful, and focused on better PC performance.*
